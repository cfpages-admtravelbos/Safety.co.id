# Article Catalog — safety.co.id

## Scope

This catalog plans 120 Indonesian-language knowledge assets across 20 parent topics for an occupational and building-safety hub. It complements the existing commerce site; it does not authorize hazardous work or replace a site assessment, approved procedure, competent person, engineer, clinician, regulator, manufacturer, or emergency service.

Domain boundaries:

- neutral knowledge, control-system education, decision support, and verification guidance belong to this catalog;
- SKU, price, stock, quotation, checkout, and product-detail intent belongs to `/shop/`, `/produk/`, and the existing commercial categories;
- no city-swapped brief is allowed;
- exact laws, clauses, limits, intervals, licenses, classifications, calculations, medical claims, or operating steps remain draft-only until the applicable review gates in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md` pass;
- articles explain what to verify and when to stop/escalate; they do not provide universal permits, rescue plans, live-work procedures, medical treatment, bypasses, or improvised repairs.

Catalog statistics: **20 topics; 120 briefs; 6 briefs per topic; 12 P0 first-cluster assets.**

Priority legend:

- **P0:** first bounded publication cluster; release only as one reviewed and internally linked system.
- **P1:** high-value foundation or serious-risk support after the first cluster proves quality and review capacity.
- **P2:** important breadth requiring discipline evidence and maintenance ownership.
- **P3:** specialist, evidence-intensive, or later-stage asset; publish only when real source material and reviewer capacity exist.

## First-wave rationale and measurement

`W1-Foundation` contains the 12 P0 assets identified in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md`. It forms one management loop: governance → hazard identification → control hierarchy/register → permit/PPE/height planning → competence/procurement → assurance → incident learning → emergency readiness. Every asset links upward to its topic hub and laterally to the next decision in the loop.

Before release, all 12 must pass their named K3, legal, discipline, engineering, health, privacy, and field-usability gates as applicable. Monitor canonical indexation, impressions separated by intent, diagram/checklist completion or meaningful engagement, internal-link traversal, reviewer corrections, qualified enquiries with originating page/intent, and same-domain query/outline overlap. Rankings alone do not authorize the next wave.
